# My agent: smart-pantry

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks discover recipes, track pantry ingredients, and plan meals with a catalog of recipes and generated dish visuals.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User dietary restrictions, allergies, and pantry inventory history
- Tools: Recipe lookup and pantry inventory management
- Catalog/UI: Collection of recipes and ingredients rendered as cards/tables
- Image gen: Visuals of completed dishes or meal presentations
- Sandbox: Nutrition and serving scale calculations

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
